## Engine and Performance

The Honda Activa 6G runs on a **109cc HET (Honda Eco Technology) engine** that delivers smooth, predictable power. It is tuned for Nizamabad's mix of city traffic, narrow lanes, and occasional highway stretches to Armoor or Bodhan.

The TVS Jupiter 125 has a **125cc engine** — slightly more powerful on paper. You will feel the difference on open roads or when carrying a pillion, but inside busy Nizamabad traffic near Station Road or Super Market area, both feel nearly identical.

**Winner: Draw.** Activa wins on smoothness and reliability. Jupiter wins on raw power.

---

## Mileage — The Number Every Nizamabad Rider Cares About

Petrol prices in Nizamabad have been above ₹100/litre. Mileage is everything.

- **Honda Activa 6G real-world mileage:** 52–56 km/l in city, 58–62 km/l on highway
- **TVS Jupiter 125 real-world mileage:** 50–54 km/l in city, 58–62 km/l on highway

Both scooters deliver similar fuel economy in real conditions. If you ride mostly within Nizamabad city, the Activa has a slight edge because its lighter engine is easier on fuel in stop-start traffic.

**Winner: Honda Activa 6G** — marginally better in city conditions typical of Nizamabad.

---

## Comfort and Ride Quality on Nizamabad Roads

Anyone who rides regularly on Nizamabad roads knows the challenge — good stretches near NH-44 and some rough patches in older areas of the city. Both scooters handle this decently.

The **Honda Activa 6G** has a well-tuned telescopic fork at the front and 3-step adjustable spring-loaded hydraulic shock absorber at the rear. The seat is wide and comfortable for the typical Nizamabad commute of 10–25 km per day.

The **TVS Jupiter 125** has a bit more cushioning in the seat and marginally more ground clearance, which helps on broken roads near Dichpally or rural areas.

**Winner: TVS Jupiter 125** — slightly better for rough road comfort.

## Storage Space

This matters a lot in Nizamabad where many riders carry bags, tiffin boxes, and groceries.

- **Activa 6G:** 18-litre under-seat storage + front hook
- **Jupiter 125:** 22-litre under-seat storage + front mobile charging USB port + glove box

The Jupiter 125 wins clearly here. The extra 4 litres is enough to fit a full-face helmet or a large shopping bag.

**Winner: TVS Jupiter 125**
