## Features & Specifications: What Makes the SP 125 Stand Out?

This Honda motorcycle isn't just about efficiency; it's also packed with features that enhance the riding experience.

### Engine and Performance

*   **Engine:** A refined 124cc, 4-stroke, SI engine with PGM-FI. It produces healthy power and torque for its segment, making overtakes easy and city riding smooth.
*   **Silent Start with ACG:** The ACG starter motor eliminates the conventional starter gear mesh, resulting in a smooth and silent engine start. It's a small detail that makes a big difference in daily use.

### Design and Technology

*   **LED Headlamp:** A bright and stylish LED headlamp not only looks premium but also offers superior illumination, especially useful for night rides on less-lit rural roads.
*   **Fully Digital Instrument Cluster:** This advanced console displays a wealth of information, including real-time mileage, average mileage, distance to empty, gear position indicator, and a service due indicator.
