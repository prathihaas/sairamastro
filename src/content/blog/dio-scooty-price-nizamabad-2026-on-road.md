### What Makes Up the On-Road Price?

Buying a two-wheeler isn't just about the sticker price. The on-road price is the total amount you pay to legally ride your vehicle on the roads of Telangana. Here are the main components:

1.  **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the base price of the vehicle, set by Honda. It varies by variant (Standard, Deluxe, Repsol Edition) and any special features.
2.  **RTO (Regional Transport Office) Charges:** These are registration fees and road tax levied by the Telangana government. These charges can vary slightly based on the vehicle's price and engine capacity. For a Dio, they are a significant part of the on-road cost.
3.  **Insurance:** Mandatory third-party insurance is required by law, and comprehensive insurance is highly recommended for your peace of mind. The premium depends on the coverage, the Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the scooter, and the insurance provider. We can help you get competitive quotes right here at Sairam Honda.
4.  **Handling/Logistics Charges:** These cover the cost of transporting the vehicle from the factory to our dealership in Nizamabad.
5.  **Accessories (Optional but common):** Many customers opt for essential accessories like a seat cover, floor mat, body guards, or even a helmet. While optional, these are often factored into the total budget.

At our Nizamabad showroom, the question we hear most often is about the 'total' cost, not just the base price. We understand that transparency is key, which is why we break down every component for you.
