## How Sairam Honda Handles Complaints and Feedback

No business is perfect, and sometimes things don't go as smoothly as planned. Whether it's a minor delay in service, a misunderstanding about a feature, or an issue with documentation, we recognize that concerns happen. What truly defines a dealership is how it responds.

When a customer raises an issue, either in person, over the phone, or through an online review, our process is clear:

### Acknowledge Immediately
We ensure the customer feels heard and understood. We don't dismiss concerns.

### Investigate Thoroughly
Our team, from the sales manager to the service head, looks into the specifics of the situation. We pull records, speak to staff involved, and understand the full context.

### Communicate Openly
We keep the customer informed about our investigation and what steps we are taking.

### Resolve Effectively
Our goal is always to find a fair and satisfactory resolution. This might involve a re-service, a clarification, or an apology and corrective action.

### Learn and Improve
Every complaint is an opportunity to strengthen our processes and prevent similar issues in the future. We often conduct internal training based on feedback received.

We believe this transparent approach is why, despite any occasional issues, our overall customer feedback remains strong and positive. It builds trust, which is the foundation of our long-standing relationship with the Nizamabad community.
