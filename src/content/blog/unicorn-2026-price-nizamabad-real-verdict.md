## Understanding On-Road Price Components in Telangana

### Ex-Showroom Price
This is the manufacturer's price before any taxes or additional charges. It's the base cost of the bike.

### RTO Registration Charges (Road Tax)
In Telangana, the Road Transport Office (RTO) charges a registration fee and road tax. This is a significant part of the on-road price and varies based on the bike's engine capacity and ex-showroom price. For a 160cc bike like the Unicorn, these charges are fixed by the state government and are non-negotiable.

### Insurance
Comprehensive bike insurance is mandatory by law. We offer various insurance options to protect your Honda Unicorn against theft, accidents, and natural calamities. The cost can vary based on the insurance provider and the coverage period (typically 1 or 3 years).

### Handling and Logistics Charges
These are charges for transporting the bike from the factory to our showroom and preparing it for delivery. While a small component, it's part of the final on-road price.
