### On-Road Price vs. Ex-Showroom Price: What Nizamabad Buyers Need to Know

It's important to differentiate between the ex-showroom price and the on-road price. The ex-showroom price is what the manufacturer charges, but the on-road price includes mandatory charges like Road Tax (RTO), insurance, and often basic accessories. In Telangana, these charges can add a significant amount. For instance, a bike with an ex-showroom price of ₹80,000 might easily push past ₹95,000 or even ₹1,00,000 on-road in Nizamabad. This is why we always discuss the full cost with our customers upfront.
