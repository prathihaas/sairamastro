### Factors Influencing the Final Price

Several elements contribute to the final on-road price of your Honda SP 160:

*   **Variant Choice:** The SP 160 comes in two main variants: Single Disc and Twin Disc. The twin-disc version, offering better braking performance, naturally costs a bit more.
*   **RTO Charges:** The Regional Transport Office (RTO) charges in Telangana are a percentage of the vehicle's ex-showroom price. These are non-negotiable and cover your vehicle's registration, number plate, and road tax. We handle all the paperwork for you at Sairam Honda, making the process smooth.
*   **Insurance:** Comprehensive insurance is mandatory. The premium depends on the insurer, the coverage period (typically 1 or 3 years for third-party, 1 year for own damage), and any add-ons you choose. We work with leading insurance providers to get you the best quotes.
*   **Accessories:** While optional, many customers opt for essential accessories like seat covers, tank pads, or crash guards, which add to the overall cost.
*   **Extended Warranty:** An extended warranty offers peace of mind beyond the standard warranty period and is a worthwhile investment for long-term ownership.

### Understanding EMI Calculations

Your Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) depends on a few factors:

*   **Loan Amount:** The total amount you borrow after your down payment.
*   **Interest Rate:** The annual interest charged by the lender.
*   **Loan Tenure:** The repayment period, typically ranging from 12 to 48 months.

A higher down payment generally means a lower loan amount and thus lower EMIs. Longer tenures reduce monthly payments but increase the total interest paid over time.
