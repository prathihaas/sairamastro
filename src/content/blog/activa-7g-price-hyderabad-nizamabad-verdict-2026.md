### What Makes Up the On-Road Price?

1.  **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the base price set by Honda. It will be very close to the "Activa 7G price in Hyderabad" you might see online.
2.  **RTO Registration:** The Road Transport Office (RTO) charges for vehicle registration. In Telangana, this is a percentage of the ex-showroom price and varies slightly based on the vehicle's cost and engine capacity. Nizamabad RTO charges will apply here.
3.  **Insurance:** Mandatory third-party insurance, plus optional comprehensive insurance. The cost depends on the insurer and the coverage chosen. Our team can help you get competitive quotes.
4.  **TCS (Tax Collected at Source):** A small percentage of the ex-showroom price.
5.  **Handling/Logistics Charges:** These are dealer-specific and cover the cost of transporting the scooter from the factory, pre-delivery inspection, and other operational expenses.
