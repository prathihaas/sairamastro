## Understanding On-Road Price Before Visiting a Motorcycle Showroom

One of the most common questions we get at our Nizamabad showroom is about the "on-road price." It's crucial to understand that the price you see advertised (ex-showroom) is not what you'll pay. The on-road price includes several components specific to Telangana:

*   **Ex-Showroom Price:** The basic cost of the vehicle.
*   **RTO Charges:** Road Tax and Registration charges, which vary by vehicle type and engine capacity. These are set by the Telangana Transport Department.
*   **Insurance:** A mandatory third-party liability insurance, often with an option for comprehensive coverage. This can vary based on the insurer and coverage period.
*   **Handling Charges/Logistics:** Sometimes included, these cover transportation from the factory to the dealership.
*   **Accessories (Optional):** Things like seat covers, footrests, guards, etc., which you might opt for.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should I ask when I visit a two-wheeler dealership?
When visiting a two-wheeler dealership, ask for the on-road price breakdown, available finance options (EMI, down payment, interest rates), current offers, test ride availability, and details about after-sales service, warranty, and AMC packages. Also, inquire about delivery timelines and required documents for purchase.

### How can I get the best deal on a new bike in Nizamabad?
To get the best deal, compare offers from different authorized dealers, especially during festival seasons when manufacturers and dealers provide special discounts. Negotiate politely, ask about free accessories, and thoroughly understand all financing options. Checking for exchange bonuses if you have an old vehicle can also help.

### What documents are needed to buy a new two-wheeler in Telangana?
Typically, you'll need ID proof (Aadhaar card, Passport, Driving License), address proof (Aadhaar card, Utility Bill), income proof (salary slips, bank statements for salaried; IT returns for self-employed), and passport-sized photographs. The exact requirements might vary slightly based on the finance provider.

### Is it better to buy a scooter or a motorcycle for daily commute in Nizamabad?
For daily commutes in Nizamabad, both scooters and motorcycles have advantages. Scooters like the Honda Activa or Dio offer gearless convenience, under-seat storage, and easy maneuverability in traffic, making them popular for city use. Motorcycles like the Honda Shine or SP 125 provide better fuel efficiency, more stable highway riding, and a generally more robust feel, suitable for longer commutes or rural roads. Your choice depends on personal preference and specific usage patterns.
