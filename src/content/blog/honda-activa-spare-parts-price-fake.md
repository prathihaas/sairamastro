## How to Identify Genuine vs. Fake Activa Parts

Spotting a counterfeit part can be tricky, but knowing what to look for can save you a lot of trouble and money. At our Nizamabad showroom, the question we hear most often is about the authenticity of parts, especially for the popular Activa scooter.

### Packaging and Labeling

Genuine Honda parts come in professional, high-quality packaging. Look for:

*   **Honda Logo:** A clear, crisp Honda logo. Fakes often have blurry or discolored logos.
*   **Part Number:** Every genuine part has a unique part number. Cross-reference this if you can.
*   **Hologram Sticker:** Honda often uses holographic security stickers. These should change color and pattern when tilted.
*   **Language and Spelling:** Genuine packaging will have correct English and sometimes local languages. Misspellings are a red flag.
*   **Barcode:** A scannable barcode is usually present on genuine packaging.

### Part Quality and Finish

Once out of the package, the part itself should speak volumes:

*   **Material Quality:** Genuine components are made from high-grade materials. Fakes might feel lighter, look discolored, or have rough edges.
*   **Finish:** A genuine part will have a smooth, consistent finish. Imperfections, scratches, or poor paint jobs are indicators of a fake.
*   **Weight:** Often, counterfeit parts are lighter than their genuine counterparts due to cheaper materials.
*   **Markings:** Genuine parts usually have Honda's branding or part numbers etched or stamped onto the component itself, not just printed.
