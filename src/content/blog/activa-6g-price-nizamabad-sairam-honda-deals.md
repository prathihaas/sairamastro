### Activa 6G On-Road Price in Nizamabad: What's Included?

The on-road price for your Honda Activa 6G in Nizamabad is a sum of these factors:

1.  **Ex-showroom Price:** This is the manufacturer's suggested retail price, excluding taxes and other local charges. It's the base price of the scooter.
2.  **RTO (Regional Transport Office) Charges:** These are the charges for vehicle registration, road tax, and number plate. In Telangana, these charges are a significant part of the final cost and vary slightly based on the scooter's ex-showroom price.
3.  **Insurance:** Comprehensive motor insurance is mandatory. The cost depends on the insurer and the type of coverage you choose (e.g., 1-year own damage + 5-year third party, or 5-year comprehensive).
4.  **Handling/Logistics Charges:** These cover the cost of transporting the vehicle from the factory to our showroom in Nizamabad.
5.  **Accessories (Optional but Recommended):** Things like seat covers, floor mats, body guards, and helmet can add to the final cost, but many customers opt for them for protection and comfort.
